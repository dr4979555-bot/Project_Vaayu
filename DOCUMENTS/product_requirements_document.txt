1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WeatherGPT is an intelligent, voice-first, multilingual conversational platform 
designed to unify India's fragmented meteorological datasets (IMD AWS telemetry, 
Doppler Weather Radars, INSAT-3D/3DR satellite products, and NWP models like 
GFS/WRF/NCUM) into actionable, hyper-local natural language intelligence.

During extreme weather crises (cyclones, cloudbursts, heatwaves, flash floods), 
traditional text-heavy synoptic bulletins fail to reach rural citizens, local 
administrators, and farmers in time. WeatherGPT bridges this gap across 22 Indian 
languages via conversational voice/text, adhering to a "Zero-Hallucination" 
deterministic standard, absolute cryptographic security for disaster alerts, and a 
cost structure that reduces LLM inference expenses by over 90% compared to 
traditional RAG chatbots.

2. PROBLEM ANALYSIS & ROOT CAUSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Data Fragmentation: Weather data is siloed across Mausam, Meghdoot (agro), 
  Damini (lightning), and regional radar portals.
- Cognitive Overload: Common users and emergency responders cannot interpret 
  isobar maps, reflectivity charts (dBZ), or raw GRIB2/NetCDF NWP tensors.
- Language & Accessibility Barrier: Bulletins are primarily in English/Hindi and 
  lack real-time voice synthesis for non-literate agricultural workers.
- The "Hallucination" Danger in Weather: Standard LLMs (e.g., vanilla GPT-4 or 
  Llama) hallucinate numerical meteorological metrics (e.g., inventing rain 
  quantities or cyclone trajectories), posing catastrophic life-safety risks.
- Cost Prohibitions: Naive LLM wrapper implementations cost upwards of $0.005 
  per query, making national-scale public dissemination economically infeasible.

3. TARGET PERSONAS & CORE USE CASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Smallholder Farmer (Kisan):
   - Needs: Hyper-local rain outlooks, humidity, and agro-advisories for spraying, 
     irrigation, and harvesting.
   - Channel: Low-bandwidth voice conversation in local dialects (e.g., Marathi, 
     Bhojpuri, Telugu).
2. Disaster Management Officer (NDRF / SDMA / District Collector):
   - Needs: Fast spatial queries on rainfall thresholds, population at risk, and 
     Common Alerting Protocol (CAP v1.2) warnings.
   - Channel: Web dashboard with natural language GIS querying and alert generation.
3. Urban Commuter & Smart City Administrator:
   - Needs: 0-3 hour nowcasts, localized waterlogging alerts, and heatwave warnings 
     at ward/pincode level.
   - Channel: WhatsApp bot, PWA mobile application, voice assistant.
4. Coastal Fisherfolk & Marine Operators:
   - Needs: High-seas wind speeds, wave heights (INCOIS feeds), port danger signals.
   - Channel: Voice alerts, compressed offline SMS fallback.
5. Climate Researcher & Analyst:
   - Needs: Historical trend synthesis and anomaly detection across 30+ years of 
     gridded IMD data.
   - Channel: Conversational data exploration with natural language-to-SQL/Python.

4. SCOPE OF REQUIREMENTS (P0 - P2)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[P0] MANDATORY FOR HACKATHON PROTOTYPE (Winning Baseline)
- Deterministic Tool-Calling Engine: LLMs NEVER generate numbers; they only 
  translate verified JSON from IMD/Open-Meteo/PostGIS into natural language.
- Real-Time Weather Retrieval: Lat/Lon and ward-level resolution for temperature, 
  precipitation probability, wind, humidity, and Air Quality Index (AQI).
- Multilingual Voice-to-Voice: Speech input and output in at least 3 languages 
  (English, Hindi, and 1 regional language e.g., Tamil/Bengali) using free-tier 
  Bhashini or open-source Whisper/Indic-TTS.
- CAP v1.2 Early Warning Engine: Push alerts for severe weather (Red/Orange/Yellow 
  alerts) with cryptographic origin tags.
- Free-Tier Cloud Deployment: Fully reproducible using open-source, free-tier 
  infrastructure (Supabase, Groq/Ollama, FastHTML/React, Leaflet).

[P1] HIGH PRIORITY (Competitive Differentiators)
- Agro-Met Advisory Integration: Automated parsing and injection of ICAR/IMD 
  Meghdoot crop advisories based on the user's localized forecast.
- Lightning Strike Warnings: Integration with Damini lightning sensors providing 
  proximity-based safety alerts within a $15\text{ km}$ radius.
- Dual-Tier Caching Architecture: In-memory tile cache to serve 70%+ of queries 
  with 0 LLM token cost and sub-100ms latency.
- Map Visualization: Interactive vector map showing radar composite layers and 
  warning polygons.

[P2] SYSTEM EXTENSIONS
- Offline/Degraded Mode: SMS/USSD text compression engine for disaster scenarios 
  when mobile internet drops.
- Citizen Science Verification: Crowdsourced user reports (e.g., local hail) 
  validated automatically against radar reflectivity matrices.

5. KEY ADD-ON PRIORITIES & SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIORITY 1: SECURITY (Zero-Trust & Disaster Integrity)
- Anti-Tamper Alert Signing: Emergency warnings must carry an ECDSA (SHA-256) 
  cryptographic signature to prevent malicious panic creation or alert spoofing.
- Prompt-Injection Firewall: Strict input guardrails to reject prompt injections, 
  jailbreaks, or attempts to make the system declare fake emergencies.
- Data Sovereignty & DPDP Compliance: Zero persistence of personally identifiable 
  information (PII) or exact GPS tracks; coordinates are snapped to a coarse 
  H3 spatial hexagon (Resolution 7, $\sim 5\text{ km}$).

PRIORITY 2: RELIABILITY & ACCURACY (Zero-Hallucination)
- Ground-Truth Anchoring: Numerical forecast values are fetched directly from 
  verified observation stations (AWS) or NWP grid cells.
- Source Transparency: Every response explicitly cites the data origin and update 
  timestamp (e.g., "Source: IMD AWS Safdarjung at 14:00 IST").
- Strict Regex Post-Validation: Model output is parsed against the ground-truth 
  JSON; if any numerical value deviates, the raw structured data is output directly 
  with a deterministic template fallback.

PRIORITY 3: SCALABILITY & COST-EFFECTIVENESS
- Free-Tier Feasibility: The entire prototype is buildable at ₹0 cost using:
  * Database: Supabase Free Tier (PostgreSQL + PostGIS)
  * LLM Inference: Groq Free Tier (Llama-3.3-70B / Llama-3.1-8B)
  * Voice Engine: Digital India Bhashini APIs (Free for Indian developers)
  * Weather Data: IMD Open Portals + Open-Meteo Open Data / ECMWF Open Data
- Production Scaling Economics: Implementation of a Spatiotemporal Redis Cache 
  and a Local Quantized Small Language Model (SLM, 3B params) yielding a 96% 
  reduction in operational inference cost ($< \$0.0002$ per query).
